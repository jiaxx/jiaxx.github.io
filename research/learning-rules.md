summary: >-
  We look for learning rules that are local enough for neural circuits to implement, yet powerful enough to build useful representations from ongoing experience. Our work links [unsupervised visual experience to changes in object-recognition behavior and inferior temporal cortex](https://elifesciences.org/articles/60830), tests how well [unsupervised algorithms reproduce human real-time and lifelong learning](https://openreview.net/forum?id=c0l2YolqD2T&noteId=WuvQmP-KBPD), and shows that [visual familiarity reorganizes activity dynamics and stimulus coding](https://elifesciences.org/articles/50340) across inhibitory and excitatory cells. We now ask which local signals can improve representations without labels, how plasticity remains stable during continuous and non-stationary experience, and which roles recurrence, feedback, and inhibition play in assigning credit across a hierarchy.
